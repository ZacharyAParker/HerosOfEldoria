⚜  BY QUIET ORDER OF THE CROWN OF ELDORIA  ⚜

A Royal Summons

(of the deniable sort)
IN THE YEAR 1754 OF THE KINGDOM ERA
❧

Know this, reader.

The Kingdom of Eldoria is at peace. It is rich, comfortable and desperately bored. Scholars in their politer moments call this the Age of Decay. Three years ago seven misfits rescued Princess Aurelia from a dragon, and the ballads got every single word of it wrong. Now there is a holiday. There is licensing paperwork. There is merchandise. Adventuring has become fashionable, which is exactly when it becomes dangerous.

The Crown therefore requires… you.

The Royal Office of Miscellaneous Threats seeks field agents for problems too weird, too small or too politically awkward for the Royal Guard. The pay is modest. The glory is unavailable. The office is a converted stable. Somebody has to.

To the veterans of 1751: yes, it is happening again. No, the ballads have not gotten more accurate. Your pensions remain conditional on your silence and your experience with catastrophe makes you uniquely qualified. Old faces may return. Old debts certainly will.

To the untested: no experience required. The last lot had none and they saved the kingdom anyway, largely by accident. You will be issued a corkboard, a clerk named Miriam and one warning: death is real this time. The gods have made that joke once already and gods do not repeat material.

✦  A Decree of Preparation  ✦

Before the convening, each agent must forge their character upon D&D Beyond — the Crown's scribes cannot abide day-of paperwork. Enlist by campaign link or send word to the Knight-Commander for aid.

Report to the stable behind the Silverhaven mews.
Bring dice. Bring thy D&D Beyond character. Leave your expectations at the door.

By order of Her Royal Highness,
who was never here,

Ser Kaelan
